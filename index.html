A model proposal: a smart bike lock

This model is about a bike lock that uses GPS, not AI. Look at how it is built, then write about your own AI tool.

Proposal: BikeGuard Smart Lock

1 · Name and user24 words

My smart lock is called BikeGuard. It is for university students who ride their own bikes to school and park them outside every day.

2 · The problem today28 words

Today, many students lose their bikes on campus. Thieves can cut a normal lock in less than a minute, and the owner often finds out only after class.

3 · Feature 1 + how it helps37 words

First, BikeGuard uses GPS to find the bike. If someone takes the bike away, the lock will send its location to the owner's phone in a few seconds so that the owner can tell the police quickly.

4 · Feature 2 + how it helps33 words

Second, the lock can feel movement. When a stranger touches or moves the bike, it will make a loud sound and send a message. This helps owners stop thieves before they ride away.

5 · Why it is better25 words

BikeGuard is safer and smarter than a normal lock. With it, students can park their bikes outside all day and worry less about losing them.

Total: 147 words

What to notice

  1. The writer starts with a person and a problem. The features come after the problem.
  2. Feature 1 names the technology: BikeGuard uses GPS to find the bike. Your tool will use an AI technology from the reading.
  3. Each feature has two parts: what it does, then how it helps the user.
  4. First and Second tell the reader where each feature begins.
  5. The last part compares the lock with a normal lock.

Do not copy these sentences. Use the same five parts and the useful language, but write about your AI tool and your user.
